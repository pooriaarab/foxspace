foxspace

SpacesSync

A space holds the context for one kind of task. On a chat site, press the foxspace button by the message box, or Alt+Shift+K (Control+Shift+K on a Mac). You see the text before you send.

      Never share

      foxspace stops any message that holds one of these terms, a short private item, or an API key. One term per line, for example a passport number.

      Import

      Paste an export from foxcache, foxpack, foxprefs or foxspace, or choose its file. Items keep their level; an item with no level is "ask".

      Recent injections

      Which space went into which site, and when. A foxtrail log in this browser shows any edit.
