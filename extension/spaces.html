foxspace

SpacesSync

A space is the context for one kind of task. On a supported chat site, press the foxspace button next to the message box, or Alt+Shift+K, to add a space to your message. You see it before you send.

Public: goes in when you pick the spaceAsk: only when you tick it in the pickerPrivate: never leaves this browser

      Never share

      foxspace stops a message that holds one of these terms on any supported chat site, and tells you which. One term per line, for example a passport or phone number.

      Import

      Paste an export, or choose its file: a foxcache archive or memory list, a foxpack file, a foxprefs export or foxprefs rules. Items keep the level they had there; an item with none is "ask". A foxspace export adds its spaces. With no space chosen, foxspace makes one.
